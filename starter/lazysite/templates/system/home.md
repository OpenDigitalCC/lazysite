This site has no home page of its own yet, so lazysite is showing this one.

To replace it, write the site's home page: `index.md` at the top of the site,
from the [manager](/manager) or by any other way you publish pages. Yours is
served from then on, and this page is not.

- [Open the manager](/manager) to write pages and choose a layout and theme.
- [Read the documentation](/docs/), which every lazysite site serves.
- If an AI assistant is building the site, point it at
  [AI briefing - building sites](/docs/ai-briefing-building-sites) first.
